What "NEWAVE Certified" actually means.

Every candidate is evaluated across five pillars. No self-reported skills, no guesswork. Only candidates who meet our threshold across all five receive the NEWAVE Certified credential.

English, validated.

Not a checkbox on a LinkedIn profile. A real assessment of written communication, verbal fluency, and professional conversation. We test it, we score it, you see the result.

AI-ready.

Certified through the Anthropic Academy program. These professionals know how to use AI to multiply their output, build workflows, and automate repetitive tasks.

Remote trained.

40+ hours of structured training across six modules: career positioning, job search strategy, interview preparation, and negotiation. They understand how distributed teams operate.

Async communication, proven.

We simulate real work scenarios. Write a Slack update about a blocker. Draft a status report. Communicate a delay to a client. Claude AI evaluates clarity, structure, and tone.

Tools proficiency.

Slack, Notion, Google Workspace, Asana, Loom. Your team's daily toolkit. Our candidates already know how to use it. Less tool training during onboarding.

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before sending your request.

What is the NEWAVE Certification?

Every candidate in our pipeline completes a structured training program and passes a five-pillar evaluation: English proficiency, AI readiness, remote-work training, async communication, and tools proficiency. Only candidates who meet our threshold across all five pillars receive the NEWAVE Certified credential.

How is this different from other staffing agencies?

Most agencies source candidates and screen for skills. Every NEWAVE profile comes from NEWAVE Academy, where members train for remote work, and we validate English and AI skills before a recruiter sees the profile.

What does "AI-ready" mean?

Our candidates have completed certifications through the Anthropic Academy program. They know how to use AI tools to automate tasks, build workflows, and increase their productivity. For employers, this means hiring someone who operates at a different level from day one.

How does an intro work?

You request contact from a profile and tell us about the role. We check with the professional. If they accept, we introduce you both by email. Recruiters do not contact candidates directly.

How do you screen your candidates?

Every candidate in our network goes through our full coaching program, where we train them on how to work remotely at a professional level, communication, async workflows, time management, and client expectations. By the time they reach you, they're not just qualified for the role, they're already the ideal remote professional for your company.

What roles are in the pilot?

Business and operations roles, including Customer Success, AI Strategy, Growth, Operations, Sales, Executive Assistants, Project Management and Finance. If you need another role, tell us in the form.

What does it cost?

The pilot is free for recruiters and companies. Candidates never pay.

Do I need to handle payroll and compliance?

Hiring terms, payroll and compliance are agreed between you and the professional. NEWAVE makes the intro.

Who are the professionals?

Members of NEWAVE Academy who asked to be considered and agreed to share their profile. Names are visible. Contact details stay with NEWAVE until an intro.
